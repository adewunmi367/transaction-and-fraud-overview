# Overview
I built a Power BI dashboard to track transaction activity and identify fraudulent transaction patterns across months, merchants, devices, and countries. The dashboard highlights potential fraud risks, financial exposure, and areas that require further investigation

# Key Insights
1. High reported fraud exposure: The dashboard records $190bn in total transaction value and $100.01bn in reported fraud losses, highlighting the importance of validating and monitoring potential financial exposure.
2. Fraud fluctuates monthly: May recorded the highest displayed fraud percentage at 71%, followed by August at 63%. June recorded the lowest at 20%. These fluctuations suggest that peak months deserve closer investigation.
3. Certain merchants have high fraud rates: GTBank Transfer, PayPal Withdrawal, and POS Terminal each show a 100% fraud rate, followed by 1XBet at 86%. These merchants should be investigated alongside their transaction counts to determine the significance of the results.
4. Transaction value is spread across merchants: Several top merchants process approximately $20bn each. Comparing transaction values with fraud rates and confirmed losses can help identify where financial exposure may be concentrated.
5. Device and country patterns provide additional context: The dashboard enables analysts to explore fraud distributions by device and country, helping identify potential patterns that require further verification.

# Recommendation 

1. Investigate fraud spikes in May, August, and November.
2. Review merchants with unusually high fraud rates.
3. Validate the dashboard's fraud-loss figures and calculation methods.
4. Compare fraud rates with transaction volumes and confirmed financial losses.
5. Introduce monitoring alerts to detect unusual increases in fraud activity.

# Conclusion 
the key findings is that fraud percentages fluctuate considerably across months, while several merchants display exceptionally high fraud rates and the dashboard reports substantial fraud losses. These findings can guide risk teams toward priority areas for investigation. However, the underlying transaction records and metric definitions should be validated before making operational decisions.
